Our philosophy

Social content, for social good.

Painter.Studio is based in Tower Hamlets — an area facing critical homelessness pressures. It’s an issue we’re helping to solve.

Each month, we fund skills-training to help people into work — to rebuild their lives, and leave homelessness behind. Every project you make with us is part of that.
